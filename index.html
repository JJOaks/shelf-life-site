Use it before it goes bad.

Shelf Life keeps track of what’s in your fridge, freezer and pantry, tells you what to use first, and shares one list with everyone you live with.

For Android. Free while it’s being tested.

What it does

  • Scan to add

    Scan a barcode and the name, amount and usual shelf life fill in. Shopping trip? Scan the whole receipt.

  • Know what to use first

    Every item shows how long it has left, in words and color, and you get a reminder before it expires.

  • Share with your household

    Everyone sees the same fridge, pantry and freezer. Keep an inventory private when it’s just yours.

  • One shopping list

    Used something up? It goes on the list for everyone, so nobody buys a second gallon of milk.

  • Receipts made readable

    “OLV GRD CAESAR DRSG” becomes Olive Garden Caesar Dressing, and Shelf Life remembers it next time.

  • Easy to read and use

    Built for TalkBack and large text, with dates written out so nothing depends on color alone.

How it works

  1. Add what you haveScan barcodes or a receipt, or type it in. Pick the fridge, freezer or pantry it lives in.
  2. Invite your householdShare an inventory, and everyone’s changes show up for everyone.
  3. Use it in timeCheck what to use first, get a reminder, and let used-up items fill the shopping list.
