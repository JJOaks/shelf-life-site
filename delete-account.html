Delete your Shelf Life account

You can delete your Shelf Life account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

Have a paid plan? Deleting your account doesn’t stop Google Play from charging you. First cancel it in the Play Store: tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Shelf Life → Cancel.

In the app

  1. Open Shelf Life and tap your profile picture at the top of the home screen.
  2. Tap Account & security.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, enter your password, and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. You’ll be signed out.

Changed your mind? Sign in again within 14 days and choose to keep your account.

Without the app

Email shelflife.software@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Shelf Life account”. Include your username if you know it. We’ll confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm it’s really you before deleting anything.

What’s deleted, and what’s kept

InformationWhat happens

Email, password, username, display nameDeleted after 14 days.

Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCatKept by them as required for tax and accounting. Ask us and we’ll ask RevenueCat to delete theirs where the law allows.

Notifications other people already received (for example, that you left a group) may still mention your name until they clear them.
